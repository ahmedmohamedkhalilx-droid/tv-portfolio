    Collection details

    Unparalleled Density. Extraordinary Luster.
    The Art of Giza 45 Mastery

    TESSERA Prestige Basic Line is woven in Egypt from 100% Giza 45 cotton, the rarest and finest of all Egyptian cottons, in a 600 thread count sateen.

    The dense weave gives the cloth real substance and a luminous, silken surface that drapes with structure rather than falling flat.

    Basic Line is left plain throughout, with no embroidery or contrast border, so the cloth and its lustre carry the whole look.

    • 100% Giza 45 Egyptian cotton, extra-long staple
    • 600 thread count, high-density sateen weave
    • 165 gsm
    • Sateen finish with a mirror-like sheen and a silk-smooth hand
    • Micro-breathable, so it holds an even temperature through the night
    • Heirloom durability, tested past 500 washes
    • Available in Baby Blue, Beige, Brown, Burgundy and Dark Blue
    • Woven and finished in Egypt

    OEKO-TEX® STANDARD 100

    Certified free from harmful substances and tested safe for prolonged contact with skin.

    Care Instructions

    • Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle
    • Tumble dry low
    • Do not bleach
    • Iron on low if needed
