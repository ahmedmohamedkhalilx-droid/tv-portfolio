    Artisan Essential

    Collection details

    The Gateway to Genuine Luxury — TESSERA Essential™ brings the legendary comfort of 100% Egyptian Cotton within reach.

    Upgrade your everyday sleep with TESSERA Essential™ — 100% Egyptian Cotton with the crisp feel of 200 thread count percale.

    Designed for a cool, breathable night's rest, this percale weave helps promote airflow and delivers a naturally fresh, smooth surface — ideal for warm climates, summer nights, and year-round comfort.

    With a clean, matte texture and a refined drape, TESSERA Essential™ brings practical elegance to any bed. It's a smart choice for first homes, rentals, and everyday stocking-up — offering comfort, easy care, and long-lasting performance at an accessible price.

    Specification

    • Collection: TESSERA Essential™
    • Material: 100% Egyptian Cotton
    • Thread Count: 200 TC
    • Weave: Percale (lightweight, crisp)
    • Benefits: Highly breathable, lightweight, authentic cotton
    • Certification: OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 (safe for human use)
    • Care: Machine washable, easy iron
    • Best For: Everyday use, summer climate
    • Colourways: Five — Gold, Blue, Sage Green, Silver and Café
    • Origin: Made in Egypt 🇪🇬

    OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 certified: free from harmful substances and tested safe for prolonged contact with skin.

    Care Instructions

    • Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle
    • Tumble dry low
    • Do not bleach
    • Easy iron, on low if needed
