    Our Story

    Weaving a new era of Egyptian luxury

    It began with a dream: the gold of our soil belongs to the hands that cultivate it. Tessera is a movement to restore Egypt's rightful place at the summit of global textile excellence.

    7,000 Years of cotton mastery
    ≥90% Quality consistency

    Crafted from earth & woven for comfort

    Making Egyptian cotton a finished luxury, not a raw export

    Founded by textile engineers and operations leaders, Tessera bridges traditional craftsmanship with contemporary design and modern supply chains. From feasibility to final finish, we oversee every step — weaving technology, quality control, and logistics.

    While honoring 7,000 years of cotton mastery, we pioneer the integration of TENCEL™, Modal, and Bamboo Viscose — sustainable fibers that enhance softness, moisture management, and environmental responsibility.

    25+ Years of combined expertise
    3 Global certifications — OEKO-TEX®, FSC®, EU Ecolabel
    5★ Hospitality-grade quality
    100% Designed & woven in Egypt

    Our Vision

    The gold of our soil, woven in Egypt

    We are dedicated to reclaiming the legacy of Egyptian cotton — fusing ancient mastery with the future of sustainable science, so that what is grown in our soil is woven, designed, and branded in the heart of Egypt, and carried to the homes of the world.

    Our Mission

    From raw cotton to finished luxury

    To restore Egypt's rightful place at the summit of global textile excellence — not as a supplier of raw cotton, but as a creator of finished luxury. True luxury is born when heritage meets responsibility, and when what you bring into your home reflects both beauty and belief.

    Our core values

    Guided by four principles in everything we weave.

    Craftsmanship

    From feasibility to final finish, every step overseen by engineers who understand textiles as engineered comfort.

    Integrity

    On time, on spec, and on budget — for 5-star hospitality and discerning households alike.

    Sustainability

    Certified mills, responsible fibers, and compliance with OEKO-TEX®, FSC®, and EU Ecolabel standards.

    National pride

    Bringing the heart of Egypt to the homes of the world — woven, designed, and branded at home.
